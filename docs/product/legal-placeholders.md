# Legal Pages (Prompt 40 §166)

Routes `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/acceptable-use`,
`/legal/security`, `/legal/subprocessors` exist as **labelled DRAFT
placeholders**. They reserve structure only:

- They are not legal advice.
- They are not finalized policies and are not "in force".
- The operating organization must substitute counsel-approved text
  before any public launch.

The Acceptable Use placeholder mirrors the product's enforced rules:
authorized testing only, attestation, protected-target exclusions
(§167–§174).
