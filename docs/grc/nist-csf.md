# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 in ZeroSweep (Prompt 26)

Shipped catalog version: NIST_CSF 2.0 (GV/ID/PR/DE/RS/RC functions).
Subcategory IDs/titles are the published reference structure (NIST
CSF 2.0 is public).

Mapping notes:

- GV (Govern) is administrative — policy, risk-governance and
  vendor controls with manual evidence.
- ID.AM (asset management) maps to the asset-inventory signal;
  ID.RA to the risk register; ID.IM to continuous improvement
  (monitoring drift).
- PR.AA (identity & access) — MFA/SSO/access-review controls;
  PR.DS (data security) — encryption controls; PR.AT — training
  (manual).
- DE.CM (continuous monitoring) — the monitoring engine itself;
  DE.AE — incident process (manual).
- RS/RC — incident and recovery controls: DR-test evidence maps
  RS.IM/RC.RP partially; the process itself is manual evidence.

CSF is a voluntary framework; ZeroSweep reports assessment states
per subcategory, **not a maturity certification**.
