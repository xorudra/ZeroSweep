# NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 in ZeroSweep (Prompt 26)

Shipped catalog version: NIST_800_53 Rev. 5 (representative controls
across AC, AU, CA, CM, CP, IA, IR, RA, SA, SC, SI families). Control
IDs/titles are the published reference structure (public NIST
material). The full catalog (1,000+ controls/enhancements) is
imported per organization via the framework import API; the shipped
subset covers the controls the platform can evidence or track.

Mapping notes:

- IA-2 maps from MFA-ENFORCEMENT (PARTIAL — mechanism details are
  organization-specific); IA-5 from credential/secret-hygiene
  signals.
- AU-2/AU-6 map to audit-logging controls; SI-2/SI-4 to
  vulnerability management and monitoring; RA-5 to scan coverage.
- CM-6/CM-7 map to secure-configuration controls; SC-8/SC-13 to
  encryption controls; CP-4/CP-9 to backup/DR controls.
- CA-2/CA-5, IR-4, SA-*, PM-adjacent controls are process controls:
  manual evidence.

800-53 baselines (FedRAMP etc.) are authorized by external
assessors/authorizing officials. ZeroSweep supplies evidence;
**it does not grant authorization or ATO.**
