# First Report (Prompt 40 §154)

1. Open the completed scan → Findings. Findings are normalized and
   deduplicated; candidates are never presented as confirmed issues.
2. Reports → New report: pick format (PDF/HTML/JSON/CSV), review the
   redaction preview, approve, and finalize — finalization creates an
   immutable, hash-stamped version.
3. Share with clients through the client portal (entitlement:
   CLIENT_PORTAL) or expiring share links — never by emailing raw
   evidence.
